A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Who am I?

My name is Tugba Unal, and I work in e-commerce and inventory management at Axess, a Belgium-based local commerce organisation. With over four years of experience across life sciences, data strategy, and innovation, I have developed a multidisciplinary professional background that informs my interest in the strategic and practical applications of emerging technologies. My professional experience has strengthened my understanding of operational processes, data-driven decision-making, and the role of innovation in supporting organisational development. In parallel, my interest in artificial intelligence (AI) has led me to explore its potential applications in business operations, strategic planning, and organisational transformation, with particular attention to governance, responsible implementation, and AI literacy. Pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Essex Online represents an important step in my academic and professional development. Through this programme, I aim to strengthen my technical knowledge, develop a critical understanding of AI methodologies and their implications, and acquire the competencies required to contribute to the effective and responsible integration of AI into organisational practices.
